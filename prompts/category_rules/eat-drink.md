# eat-drink

Anywhere whose primary business is **prepared food or drink served to you**.

## Belongs here

- Restaurants (all service levels), diners, quick-bites / fast food, food trucks.
- Bars, pubs, breweries, wineries/tasting rooms, cocktail lounges.
- Cafes, coffee shops, juice/smoothie bars, dessert/ice-cream/bakery storefronts.
- Catering operations and ghost kitchens.

## Does NOT belong here

- Grocery stores, markets, butchers, delis-inside-a-market → `shopping-essentials`.
  Rule of thumb: a market sells **ingredients to take home**; eat-drink serves
  **prepared food/drink**.
- A hotel that happens to have a restaurant → `lodging-vacation-rentals`, unless
  the listing IS the restaurant.
- On-the-water dinner cruises → `on-the-water` (the boat is the business).

## Edge cases

- A **brewery** that mainly produces but has a taproom → eat-drink.
- A **convenience store** with a hot-food counter is still `shopping-essentials`
  (primarily retail).
- A **bar with live music** stays eat-drink, not `events` — it's a bar first.
