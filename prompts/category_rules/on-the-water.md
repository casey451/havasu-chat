# on-the-water

Lake Havasu's defining category: businesses built around **watercraft and on-water
activity**.

## Belongs here

- Boat / jet-ski / kayak / paddleboard / pontoon rentals and dealers.
- Marinas, boat slips, fuel docks, boat ramps/launches.
- Boat & marine repair, marine mechanics, detailing, winterizing.
- Boat tours, charters, dinner cruises, fishing guides, dive/snorkel shops,
  parasail / flyboard / wakeboard operators.

## Does NOT belong here

- Lakeside **restaurants** → `eat-drink`; waterfront **hotels** → `lodging-
  vacation-rentals`. Being near the water doesn't make it on-the-water.
- **Beaches, shoreline parks, and waterfront trails** → `outdoors-parks-trails`.
  Rule of thumb: a boat or in-water activity is on-the-water; the **land/shore**
  itself is outdoors.

## Edge cases

- **Marine** repair / a boat dealership → on-the-water, NOT `auto-rv-fuel`, even
  though it's "a mechanic / a dealer." Engines on water belong here.
- A retailer selling only boating **accessories** with no rental/service could be
  `shopping-essentials`; if it rents or services craft, on-the-water wins.
