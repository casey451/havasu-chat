# auto-rv-fuel

Vehicles and fuel: selling, fueling, fixing, and servicing cars, trucks, RVs, and
motorcycles.

## Belongs here

- Gas / fuel stations, EV charging.
- Auto / RV / motorcycle repair, oil change, tires, brakes, transmission, body
  shops, smog/emissions, detailing, car washes.
- Car / truck / RV / motorcycle dealers and rentals; auto-parts stores; towing.

## Does NOT belong here

- **Boat / marine** repair and boat dealers → `on-the-water` (engines on water go
  there, not here).
- **RV parks / campgrounds** where you stay overnight → `lodging-vacation-rentals`.
  Rule of thumb: **service or sell the RV → auto-rv-fuel; sleep in it on their lot
  → lodging.**
- Auto **insurance / financing** offices → `professional-services`.

## Edge cases

- A dealership that **sells** RVs is auto-rv-fuel; the RV **resort** down the road
  is lodging.
- Auto-parts **retail** stays here (not `shopping-essentials`) — it's part of the
  vehicle-service ecosystem.
