# events

The **provider side** of events: attractions, venues, and entertainment places.
(Calendar happenings are a separate event feed — this is the business/venue.)

## Belongs here

- Tourist attractions, sightseeing, tours (land-based), museums, art galleries.
- Event / wedding venues, banquet halls, theaters, concert & music venues.
- Entertainment: arcades, escape rooms, mini-golf, go-karts, casinos, family fun
  centers.

## Does NOT belong here

- Restaurants and **bars with live music** → `eat-drink` (a bar is a bar first).
- **On-the-water** tours and charters → `on-the-water`.
- **Sports / fitness** recreation, bowling alleys treated as leagues → `classes-
  sports-recreation`.
- Hotels hosting events → `lodging-vacation-rentals`.

## Edge cases

- Rule of thumb: **a place you visit for an attraction, or a venue you book → here;
  a dated happening → the calendar event feed, not a provider category.**
- A **concert hall** is events; a **live-music bar** is eat-drink.
