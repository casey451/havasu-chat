# home-property-services

Trades and services performed **on a home or property**.

## Belongs here

- Contractors, handymen, remodelers, roofers, painters, flooring, fencing,
  garage doors, windows, concrete, solar install.
- Plumbing, electrical, HVAC, appliance repair.
- Landscaping, lawn, tree, pool service, pest control, cleaning, junk removal.
- Self-storage facilities and moving companies (property services; the canonical
  set has no standalone storage bucket).

## Does NOT belong here

- Real-estate brokerages, agents, **property management offices**, appraisers →
  `professional-services`. Rule of thumb: **the work happens at your house →
  home-property; it happens at their office/desk → professional**.
- Furniture / hardware / home-improvement **retail stores** → `shopping-
  essentials` (a store that sells you product, not a service crew).

## Edge cases

- A solar **installer** is home-property; a solar **financing** office is
  professional-services.
- A store that also installs (e.g. flooring retail + install) → judge by primary:
  showroom-led → shopping; crew-led → home-property.
