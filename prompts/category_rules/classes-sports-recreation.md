# classes-sports-recreation

**Active recreation and instruction** — moving, playing, and learning.

## Belongs here

- Gyms, fitness centers, CrossFit, personal trainers.
- Yoga / pilates / dance / spin / martial-arts **studios**.
- Racquet sports (tennis, pickleball), bowling, recreation centers, sports leagues.
- Lessons & education: swim/music/art lessons, tutoring, kids camps, childcare,
  daycare, preschools.

## Does NOT belong here

- Massage, physical therapy, chiropractic, med-spa **treatment/grooming** →
  `health-wellness-care`. Rule of thumb: **instruction or active recreation is
  here; bodily treatment or grooming is care.**
- Passive outdoor space — parks, trails, golf **courses** → `outdoors-parks-
  trails` (a golf course is outdoors; golf **lessons** are here).
- Libraries, museums, government → `public-civic-resources` / `events`.

## Edge cases

- A **yoga studio** is recreation; a **physical-therapy clinic** that teaches
  therapeutic yoga is `health-wellness-care`.
- Private **lessons / childcare / tutoring** are here; a public **school
  district office** is `public-civic-resources`.
