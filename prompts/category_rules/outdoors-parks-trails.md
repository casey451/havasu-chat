# outdoors-parks-trails

**Land-based** outdoor places and recreation.

## Belongs here

- Parks, beaches, shoreline parks, picnic areas, scenic lookouts, nature/wildlife
  areas.
- Hiking trails, OHV / off-road trails, mountain-bike trails.
- Golf courses and disc-golf courses.
- Bike shops, bike rentals, and biking outfitters.

## Does NOT belong here

- **Watercraft / on-water** activities (boats, kayaks, marinas, beaches *as a
  launch*) → `on-the-water`. Rule of thumb: **the boat or in-water activity is
  on-the-water; the land, shore, park, and trail are outdoors.**
- Indoor gyms, fitness/yoga **studios**, golf **instruction/simulators** →
  `classes-sports-recreation`.
- Overnight RV parks / campgrounds → `lodging-vacation-rentals`.

## Edge cases

- A **golf course** is outdoors; a golf **academy / indoor simulator / lessons**
  → classes-sports-recreation.
- A **bike shop** (rental, repair, trail outfitter) stays here under "biking,"
  not `shopping-essentials`.
