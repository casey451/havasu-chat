# public-civic-resources

Genuinely **civic, community, and government** things — kept narrow after the
WP-9 split moved paid professional services out.

## Belongs here

- Government offices and services, city/county facilities, DMV, courts, post
  offices.
- Public safety: fire, police, emergency services.
- Libraries, community centers, senior centers.
- Places of worship (churches, mosques, temples, synagogues).
- Nonprofits, charities, civic clubs, public utilities.

## Does NOT belong here

- Paid **professional offices** — legal, financial, accounting, insurance, real
  estate → `professional-services`. Rule of thumb: **civic / community /
  government / worship → here; a service you pay a professional for → professional.**
- Museums, galleries, theaters, attractions, event venues → `events`.
- Private lessons, tutoring, childcare → `classes-sports-recreation`.

## Edge cases

- A **public school district office** is civic; a private tutoring/childcare
  business is classes-sports-recreation.
- A **charity** is civic; its standalone **thrift storefront** can read as
  `shopping-essentials`.
