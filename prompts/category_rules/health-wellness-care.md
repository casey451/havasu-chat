# health-wellness-care

Health, medical, and personal-care services. The canonical set has no standalone
beauty bucket, so **personal care / beauty folds in here** (mirrors the
`beauty` → health-wellness fold in the subcategory map). This causes the most
common mistakes, so judge carefully.

## Belongs here

- Medical & dental: doctors, clinics, urgent care, dentists, orthodontists,
  optometrists, chiropractors, physical therapists, mental-health/counseling.
- Specialty care: dialysis, imaging, labs, home health, hospice, pharmacies.
- Wellness: massage therapy, acupuncture, day spas, med-spas, float/sauna.
- Personal care / beauty: hair salons, barbers, nail salons, skincare/esthetics,
  tanning, lash/brow, waxing.

## Does NOT belong here

- Gyms, fitness studios, yoga/pilates studios, martial arts, personal trainers →
  `classes-sports-recreation`. Movement/instruction is recreation; treatment and
  grooming are care. (A yoga *studio* is recreation; a physical-therapy clinic
  that happens to teach therapeutic yoga is care.)
- Vitamin/supplement **retail** stores → `shopping-essentials`. A store that
  sells product is shopping; a clinic that delivers a service is care.
- Veterinarians, pet grooming, pet supply → `pets` (animal care has its own
  bucket; do not file vets under health-wellness).
- Counseling that is actually a law/financial *practice* → `professional-services`.

## Edge cases

- **Med-spa vs salon vs clinic:** all three live here — don't downgrade a med-spa
  to shopping or recreation.
- **Barber/salon vs retail:** a salon that mainly cuts hair is care even if it
  sells some product; a beauty-supply store is `shopping-essentials`.
- **Pharmacy inside a grocery/big-box:** if the listing is the pharmacy, it's
  care; if it's the grocery store, it's `shopping-essentials`.
