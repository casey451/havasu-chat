# pets

Anything centered on **animals** — care, services, and retail.

## Belongs here

- Veterinarians, animal hospitals, emergency/mobile vets.
- Grooming, boarding/kennels, doggy daycare, dog training/walking, pet sitting.
- Pet stores / supply, feed stores, aquarium/reptile specialty.
- Animal shelters, rescues, adoption.

## Does NOT belong here

- Rule of thumb: **any animal care or animal retail → pets, never elsewhere.**
- A vet is `pets`, NOT `health-wellness-care` (that bucket is human care only).
- A pet-supply store is `pets`, NOT `shopping-essentials`.

## Edge cases

- A **pet-friendly hotel** is still `lodging-vacation-rentals` — the business is
  lodging; allowing pets is just an amenity.
- A **livestock/feed** store leans pets; if it's clearly a general farm/ranch
  **hardware** retailer, `shopping-essentials` is defensible.
- **Service-animal** training → pets.
