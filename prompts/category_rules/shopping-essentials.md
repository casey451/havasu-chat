# shopping-essentials

Retail: places that **sell product to take home**, plus everyday essentials.

## Belongs here

- Grocery, supermarkets, convenience stores, markets, butchers, liquor stores.
- Clothing, boutiques, shoes, jewelry, gifts, florists.
- Furniture, home goods, hardware / home-improvement retail, electronics.
- Specialty retail: books, toys, thrift/consignment, sporting-goods stores.

## Does NOT belong here

- Prepared food / coffee / bars → `eat-drink` (serves food vs sells groceries).
- Salons, spas, pharmacies-as-service → `health-wellness-care`.
- **Pet** stores / supply → `pets` (the pets bucket owns pet retail).
- Auto-parts retail → `auto-rv-fuel`.

## Edge cases

- Rule of thumb: **sells you product to take home → shopping; performs a service
  → its service category.** A sporting-goods STORE is shopping; a gym is
  `classes-sports-recreation`.
- A nonprofit **thrift store** run by a charity → shopping if it's the storefront
  listing; `public-civic-resources` if it's the org/mission listing.
