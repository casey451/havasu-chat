# professional-services

Resident- and business-facing **professional/office services** — the "who do I
call for X" pros. Split out of `public-civic-resources` in the WP-9 taxonomy
rework, so legacy data frequently still files these as civic or shopping.

## Belongs here

- Legal: law firms, attorneys, title companies, paralegal/notary services.
- Financial: accountants/CPAs, tax preparers, bookkeeping, financial advisors,
  banks and credit unions, mortgage/loan brokers.
- Insurance: agencies and brokers (auto, home, life, health).
- Real estate: brokerages, individual agents, property management offices,
  appraisal services.
- Business/personal services delivered from an office: marketing/ad agencies,
  consultants, staffing/employment agencies, print/sign shops serving businesses,
  IT/managed-services providers.

## Does NOT belong here

- A bank's **ATM-only** kiosk or a civic office → keep civic things in
  `public-civic-resources` (library, worship, government, community centers).
- Home trades (plumber, electrician, HVAC, landscaping, contractor) →
  `home-property-services`. Rule of thumb: if the work happens at *your house*,
  it's home-property; if it happens at *their office/desk*, it's professional.
- Medical/dental/therapy practices → `health-wellness-care` (a clinic is care,
  not a professional office, even though both are licensed professionals).
- Auto repair / mechanics / RV service → `auto-rv-fuel`.

## Edge cases

- **Real-estate vs lodging:** a brokerage or property manager that sells/manages
  homes is professional-services; a vacation-rental host or short-term-rental
  management company is `lodging-vacation-rentals`.
- **Insurance vs auto:** an insurance agency is professional-services even if it
  only writes auto policies; an auto-body or repair shop is `auto-rv-fuel`.
- A CPA who also offers payroll software is still professional-services.
