# lodging-vacation-rentals

Places to **stay overnight**, and the businesses that host/manage them.

## Belongs here

- Hotels, motels, resorts, inns, B&Bs, hostels.
- Vacation rentals / short-term rentals and STR **management/host** companies.
- RV parks, campgrounds, and glamping with overnight stays.

## Does NOT belong here

- Real-estate brokerages / agents who **sell** homes, and long-term **residential**
  property management → `professional-services`. Rule of thumb: **somewhere you
  sleep for a trip → lodging; buying or long-term-leasing property → professional.**
- A hotel's **restaurant or spa**, listed on its own → `eat-drink` /
  `health-wellness-care`.
- RV **dealers / service** → `auto-rv-fuel` (sell/fix the RV vs stay in it).

## Edge cases

- An **RV park** (overnight) is lodging; an **RV dealership** is auto-rv-fuel.
- A company that manages **short-term vacation** rentals → lodging; one managing
  **long-term residential** leases → professional-services.
