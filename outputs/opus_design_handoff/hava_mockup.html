Reading this mockup

Palette is intentionally muted. Sand/cream background, deep lake teal as the one accent, terracotta only for explicit sponsor labels. No purple gradients. No "discovering amazing experiences" loading copy. Inter for UI, Fraunces for display.
Freshness dots replace stars. Three states — green (recently verified), amber (stale, call ahead), red (not verified, treat with caution). The same logic renders on cards as a dot and on profiles as a full-width band.
< 60 days 60–180 days > 180 days
What's not on screen by design. No popups, no "save / favorite / share" trio, no badges system, no streaks, no countdown timers, no review-aggregation theater. The interface earns trust by what it refuses to do.
