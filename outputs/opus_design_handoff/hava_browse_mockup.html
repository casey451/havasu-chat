The browse hierarchy now has three layers

The user can travel from "I don't know what I want" to "this specific business" through a 4-step path that's intuitive at each step. The themed groups carry the structural load; the homepage stays calm because each group tile previews its contents inline.

Homepage → Themed group (e.g. On the Water) → Sub-category (e.g. Boat Rentals) → Profile

Power users skip the group layer entirely — the A–Z link goes straight to any of the 47 sub-categories. Ask Hava handles the "I don't know what category this is" case. Three front doors, one directory.
