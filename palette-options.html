Recommended Codeska's framing — "off-white tones with blue and warm brown accents… nautical elegance" — is essentially the current direction stated cleanly. The change vs. v2 mockup: the editorial accent shifts from terracotta-clay to a warm brown (#8a6f5a) that's quieter and less seasonal. Lowest risk because the brand identity (lake water = primary) stays intact; highest payoff because the page gets a quieter editorial feel without losing scope.

How to choose

The strongest filter is whether the palette protects the lake-as-primary-identity. Hava is named after the lake; the brand line is "Lake Havasu's local;" the mission references the place by name. Any palette that drops teal as the primary tier weakens that identity link.

By that filter: Yacht club (D) and Ocean tide (B) are both viable; Desert mirage (A) is a real alternative if you want to reposition Hava as a desert-town product first; Tuscan sunset (C) is too off-brief to be the year-round palette but could work as a seasonal evening render.

My pick is Yacht club because it commits to the existing brand direction with codeska's framework as validation, and because it changes the editorial accent from the slightly seasonal-feeling clay to a more durable warm brown — the kind of color that doesn't go out of style in 18 months. Second choice would be Ocean tide if you want to lean harder into water identity at the expense of the Sunday-paper feel.
