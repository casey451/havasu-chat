Programs & services

Meals on Wheels. Hot meals delivered almost every weekday to homebound seniors, with frozen meals for the weekend, plus a friendly wellness check at the door.

Good Morning Sunshine. A free daily check-in call to make sure seniors living alone are safe and well — and to start the day with a friendly hello. To enroll, call {{ phone }}.

Thrift Store. Hidden treasures inside the center — clothing and home goods — with proceeds supporting the center's programs.

Get involved

Come for a class, a meal, or a game of pool — everyone's welcome. To volunteer, donate, or ask about activities, call {{ phone }} or visit the Senior Center website.
