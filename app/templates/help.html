Frequently asked

Help & FAQ

How do I sign in?

Ask Hava uses passwordless sign-in. Enter your email on the sign-in page and we email you a one-time link that logs you in. There is no password to remember.

How do I save a place?

Tap the heart on any listing. Saved places live under Saved once you are signed in.

Where does the conditions data come from?

Live local sources -- USGS for lake level and water temperature, NWS for weather, AirNow for air quality, and crowd-sourced gas prices. We cache and timestamp each source, and show "Unavailable" rather than guess when a source is stale.

Something is wrong on a listing. How do I report it?

Use the "Report wrong info" link in the footer of any page, or email us at hello@havasuchat.com. To add a new business, add it to Hava.

How do I claim my business?

Open your business listing and choose Claim, or sign in and start a claim from your account. We verify ownership before handing over editing.
