EVENT

Life Jacket Exchange

Start Date
July 19, 2099
Time
10:00 AM - 12:00 PM
Location
London Bridge Beach 1340 McCulloch Blvd. N Lake Havasu City, AZ
Organizer
Mohave County Sheriff's Office Division of Boating Safety

The Mohave County Sheriff’s Office Division of Boating Safety is hosting a Life Jacket Exchange at the London Bridge Beach stage.

Water enthusiasts can exchange worn, torn or otherwise unserviceable life jackets for a new vest style life jacket free of charge.
