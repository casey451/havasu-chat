Round Robins

Weekly round robins will be scheduled soon starting in December! These activities are member-only events and invitations are sent via SignUp Genius.

These events are organized by skill levels and last about two hours with at least six games included.

Round Robin Play Guidelines

A "Round Robin" is structured form of play organized by skill levels. In the time allocated to each Round Robin session (100 minutes) you will play six games, each with a different partner and against a different pair of opponents.
