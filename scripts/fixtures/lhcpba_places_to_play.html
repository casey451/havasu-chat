Places to Play

Lake Havasu City Pickleball Association coordinates and schedules a variety of opportunities to play pickleball from recreational open play to Tournaments, Round Robins and Lessons/Clinics.

Facility Locations

Mike Delaney Pickleball Complex at Dick Samp Park

1628 Avalon Ave.
Lake Havasu City, AZ 86404

There are sixteen dedicated outdoor pickleball courts. This location has public bathrooms and a drinking fountain nearby. You will need to bring your own chair and sun protection. It's FREE to play here. There are no lights for evening play. Four additional courts (to complete the 16 court complex) opened on 2/21/23!

The Aquatic Center

Located at 100 Park Ave, Lake Havasu City, AZ 86403 at the intersection of route 95 and Mulberry Ave. It costs $3/per session to play here. The pickleball schedule fluctuates monthly at this venue.

The Ark Center

Located behind Inspire The Church
2700 Jamaica Blvd. South
Lake Havasu City, AZ 86406

Three indoor courts on a brand new floor sponsored by LHCPBA. Check the website Calendar for days and times of open play. Cost is $5/per session.
