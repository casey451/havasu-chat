Hava · home page · redesign mockup · v2 (dialed-in)

From data table to local almanac.

Side-by-side comparison of the live site against a redesign that fixes the content-leakage problems first, then re-paces the layout, palette, and component system. v2 incorporates a self-critique pass: Marquee moved below Today; ad inventory cut from 5 tiers to 4; Promoted card redesigned with a real visual differentiator; disclosure copy consolidated; moderation, performance budget, and AI policy added.

Ten things I'd fix first

Ranked by user impact. The first four are content correctness; the rest are visual/IA. Full reasoning lives in CRITIQUE_AND_REDESIGN.md.

01 Snake_case category labels

"find a health_medical" / "food_drink" / "religion_community" — raw enum slugs leak into the user-facing strip. A label table fixes this in 20 lines of Python.

critical
02 Database boilerplate in card blurbs

Every event card prints "Lap Swim on 2026-05-08 at 5:00 AM at the Lake Havasu City Aquatic Center. See full schedule and current pricing at https://..." — same template, six times, with raw URL and ISO date.

critical
03 CSV export in the Farmers Market card

Reads "Date: ... Time: 12:00 – 12:00 ... Venue: 2144 McCulloch Blvd NLake Havasu City..." — no space between N and Lake, time range start = end, label-style colons visible.

critical
04 "Tonight" includes 5 AM events

tonight() filters by date, not by hour. Today the hero event under "Tonight" is Lap Swim at 5 AM. Either gate by hour ≥ 16:00 or rename the section "Today" until evening.

critical
05 No event imagery → page reads as text-only

_provider_image_url returns None; events have no image surface yet. Only paid Spotlight cards have photos, so paid placement visually outranks editorial — the inverse of what a trusted local guide should communicate.

critical
06 Identical bullet-circle category icons

Ten categories, one SVG. Zero scannability. Replace with line glyphs (drop, fork, wrench, sun, paw, scissors, gear, leaf, hammer, compass).

high
07 Card grid monotony

Three sections of 3-up cards, all the same radius/shadow/arrow. No magazine pacing. Replace one row with a 7-day list, one with a dark inverted "On the water" block, and one with a compact list view.

high
08 Status pills rendered without data

"Open · until 6" is hard-coded. _hours_status returns a placeholder. Pills should only render when the underlying data justifies them — otherwise plain meta text.

high
09 Chat-only IA

Every chip, tile, and "see all" routes to /chat?q=... There's no browse mode. Each category should also link to /categories/{slug} with a filterable list.

high
10 Place identity is wallpapered, not used

Lake Havasu has the bridge, the channel, the sunset over Pittsburg Point. None of it is on the page. The hero glow gradient is doing the work that a single curated photo should.

medium
