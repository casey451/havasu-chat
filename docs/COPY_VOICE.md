# Ask Hava — copy voice rules

Codified from the 2026-06-10 copy audit. The voice was already ~80% consistent;
this writes the implicit rules down so they survive new surfaces.

## Who speaks where

- **Hava says "I"** on product surfaces: chat, contribute ("Tell me what
  belongs.", "Know a place I'm missing?"), 404's "Ask Hava where it went →".
- **The company says "we"** on about, help, contact, login/auth, business
  pages (/portal/*), and anything legal.
- Never both voices in one block.

## House style

- **Contract everywhere.** "We'll email you a link", not "We will send".
  The auth pages are part of the product, not a terms sheet.
- **The name goes on the button, not in every line.** One "Ask Hava" per
  screen region is plenty; eyebrows and headlines make the promise instead.
- **No system-speak on consumer pages.** Banned words in user-facing copy:
  server-rendered, volume-weighted, enriched (except the "Verified & Enriched
  Listing" product name), scarcity-priced, catalog (it's "Hava"), utility
  (breadcrumbs say the page's name: Gas, Today).
- **Say the real number/time.** "Average across all 24 tracked stations",
  "Updated 7:06 AM" — never ">6h ago" math next to a real timestamp.
- **Real data or nothing.** No fake placeholders, no empty storefronts: a
  sales surface renders only when something is sold (home Featured row), and
  empty states say something honest instead of pretending.

## Ranking disclosure (exact copy)

Whenever a list's ordering is described:
"Ranked by real public reviews — more reviews, more weight. Spots can't be
bought, and Hava never invents a rating." (Sponsored placements: "clearly
labeled" rides along wherever ads can appear.)
