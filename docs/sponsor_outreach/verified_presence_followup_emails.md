## Notes on use

- **Don't follow up beyond Day 7** unless the merchant explicitly asked for a later check-in (e.g., "ask me again next month"). The Day 7 close is the natural exit point.
- **Subject line A/B variants** worth trying if reply rates are low: "Following up on Havasu Chat", "Quick question about your business listing", "[Their business name] — Havasu Chat verified listing".
- **If they reply with "yes, set me up,"** route to the claim/onboarding flow once that's wired. For V1, "set up" means Casey manually creates the verified listing using the data he collected during the pitch.
- **If they reply with "no thanks":** hand off to the referral-ask if the conversation is cordial (see `verified_presence_referral_script.md`); otherwise just thank them and exit cleanly.
