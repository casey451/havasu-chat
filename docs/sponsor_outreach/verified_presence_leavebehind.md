# A simple way for Lake Havasu locals to find and contact local businesses.

Havasu Chat is a local-only business directory and chat tool built specifically for Lake Havasu City. People can browse categories manually or ask questions like "who fixes AC units after hours" or "best local pool service near me."

Verified Presence is the first sponsor package for local businesses.

## What Verified Presence includes

- Verified business listing inside Havasu Chat
- Business information you can control and update
- Priority appearance in relevant local chat searches
- Verified badge with updated business details
- Photos, services, hours, contact info, and description support

## What it does NOT promise

- Guaranteed leads or booked jobs
- Exclusive placement in your category
- Overnight results or inflated traffic claims

This is meant to sit alongside Google, Facebook, referrals, and your existing marketing — not replace them.
