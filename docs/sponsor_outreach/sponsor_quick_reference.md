## How customers find you

Customers usually find businesses through conversational searches instead of browsing categories manually.

Example searches:

- "good mexican food open now"
- "emergency plumber tonight"
- "boat repair open saturday"
- "where can i get stitches"
- "ac repair near me"

Hava routes from the user's question itself, not from a traditional search-results page full of listings and ads.

## What to expect

Hava traffic is intent-based, not pure volume-based. Users typically arrive because they are actively looking for a specific service, activity, or type of business in the moment.

Some categories generate steady daily searches. Others spike around weekends, holidays, tourist traffic, or seasonal demand.

You will receive a check-in around day 25 of the first month before the 30-day money-back window closes. At the end of month 1, the placement either continues normally or pauses based on your decision.
