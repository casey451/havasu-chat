# Post-launch sponsor communications

**Companion to:** `docs/sponsor_outreach/cold_email_templates.md` (the generic Hava cold-email template), `docs/sponsor_outreach/cold_email_variants_2026-05-09.md` (category-specific variants), and `docs/sponsor_outreach/reply_handlers.md` (yes / no / questions / follow-up handlers for the reply phase). Those docs cover outbound outreach and reply handling before a Spotlight goes live. This file covers the three sponsor-touch emails sent after launch: the day-of launch email, the day-25 check-in inside the money-back window, and the post-month-1 retention / pause variants. All templates use `[BRACKETS]` placeholders for fill-in details.

---

## Section 1 — Launch email

Send the same day the Spotlight placement goes live.

Hi [Business Name],

Your Hava Spotlight placement is now live as of [LAUNCH DATE].

Your business is currently appearing in the home page Spotlight section and can also show up when Hava users search for relevant things in Lake Havasu related to your category.

You can view the listing here: [LAUNCH URL or BUSINESS LISTING URL]

If you notice anything you'd like adjusted — hours, wording, phone number, description, or anything else — just reply and I'll update it.

One thing to keep in mind: Hava traffic behaves a little differently than Yelp or Google ads. The goal is to route people searching for something specific toward a relevant local business naturally, instead of sending paid-click traffic into a directory-style search flow.

You may also want to share the listing with your staff so they know what customers are seeing if somebody mentions finding you through Hava.

Thanks, Casey

---

## Section 2 — Day-25 check-in

Send manually around day 25, while the 30-day money-back window is still open. This is the most important email in this doc — it respects the money-back guarantee in spirit, not just letter.

Hi [Business Name],

Wanted to check in since it's been about 25 days since your Spotlight placement went live.

The 30-day money-back window closes in about 5 days, so this is the point where I want honest feedback if anything feels off, underwhelming, or not worth continuing. No pressure either way.

One thing that's especially useful for me to know: have you noticed any Hava-referred calls, messages, walk-ins, or customer mentions yet, even informally. A "not yet" answer is still helpful feedback on my side.

If you have any concerns before the 30-day window closes, reply here and we'll sort through them directly.

If everything looks good, no action needed and the placement just continues normally into next month.

Thanks, Casey

## Section 3 — Post-month-1 retention / renewal

Two variants. Use 3a if the day-25 check-in passed cleanly. Use 3b if the sponsor asked to step away — either at day-25 or in a separate reply.

### 3a — Continuation confirmation

Hi [Business Name],

Just confirming your Spotlight placement has rolled into month 2 and will continue normally from here. [CASEY: confirm auto-renew billing cadence vs explicit per-month renewal before sending — needs to match what was set up at launch.]

Your listing is still active in the home page Spotlight section and eligible for relevant chat-result placement as Hava users search.

Going forward, the cadence should stay pretty quiet unless you need edits or want to change anything about the listing. I'll plan to check back in around month 3 unless you reach out sooner.

Thanks, Casey

### 3b — "Let's pause" handler

Hi [Business Name],

Understood. I'll remove the Spotlight placement effective [PAUSE DATE].

I appreciate you giving it a try and helping me build out the Hava business catalog early on. If you decide later that you'd like to come back into the Spotlight rotation, just send me a note and we can pick it back up.

Thanks again for the time and feedback.

Thanks, Casey

---

## Section 4 — Send protocol

- Send the launch email the same day the Spotlight placement goes live.
- Always include the live listing URL if available before sending.
- Send the day-25 check-in manually, not automated, so replies can be handled thoughtfully.
- Track whether sponsors mention actual Hava-referred calls, messages, or walk-ins during the first month.
- If a sponsor reports zero traction or uncertainty at day 25, do not blindly continue into renewal without a real conversation first.
- Use the continuation confirmation (3a) only after the 30-day window closes cleanly without refund or pause requests.
