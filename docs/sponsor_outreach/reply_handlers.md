# Sponsor outreach — reply handlers

**Companion to:** `docs/sponsor_outreach/cold_email_templates.md` (the generic Hava cold-email template) and `docs/sponsor_outreach/cold_email_variants_2026-05-09.md` (category-specific variants). Those docs cover the initial outbound pitch. This file covers what happens after a business owner replies. All templates use `[BRACKETS]` placeholders for fill-in details.

---

## Section 1 — YES handler

Use immediately after any clear affirmative reply, even short ones like "sounds good" or "send details."

Hi [Business Name],

Sounds good. I'll get your Spotlight placement added and scheduled to go live on [LAUNCH DATE].

Before I build the listing, can you send over a few details so I have everything accurate:

- Best contact phone number
- Full street address with zip code
- Business hours
- Website URL if you have one
- Preferred email for ongoing communication if different from this one
- A short line on what you're known for or what makes you different

I'll use that information for the Spotlight description and chat-result placement so people searching through Hava can find the right fit quickly.

The terms are still the same: $59/month with a 30-day money-back guarantee if it isn't a fit for you.

Before launch, I'll send over the final description so you can reply with anything you'd like adjusted or clarified.

Thanks, Casey

## Section 2 — NO handler

## Section 3 — Asks-questions handler

A reusable Q&A bank. Pull individual answers and paste into a personal reply — don't send the whole section at once. Several answers are flagged with `[CASEY: ...]` where the answer requires a fact that should be confirmed against current product reality before sending.

**Q: What exactly does the Spotlight placement do?**

A: The Spotlight placement puts your business near the top of the home page and also gives you placement when somebody asks Hava for businesses in your category. The idea is to get people directly from a local search-style question to your business without making them dig through Google or Yelp results.

**Q: How is this different from Yelp or Google ads?**

A: Yelp and Google usually turn into comparison shopping. People bounce between ads, reviews, outdated hours, and ten different tabs before they make a decision. Hava is built around direct intent. Somebody asks something specific like "boat repair open saturday" or "good mexican food open now," and the goal is to get them to a relevant local business faster.

**Q: What kind of people are using Hava right now?**

A: Mostly local Lake Havasu residents and visitors trying to figure out where to go or who to call without searching through a bunch of directories. Common searches are restaurants, repair services, urgent care, events, and after-hours needs. [CASEY: confirm current user volume and usage numbers before giving specifics.]

**Q: What information do you need from me to get listed?**

A: Pretty simple. I just need your business phone number, address, hours, website if you have one, preferred contact email, and a short description of what you're known for. That lets me build the Spotlight listing and make sure Hava returns accurate information when somebody searches.

**Q: How do customers actually find me through Hava?**

A: They usually type questions the same way they'd text somebody local. Stuff like "who fixes jet skis today" or "quiet dinner place with kids." If your business matches the request, your listing can appear directly in the results instead of the user having to sort through multiple search sites first.

**Q: What happens if I want to cancel?**

A: You can cancel anytime. The first 30 days are covered by the money-back guarantee if you decide it isn't worth continuing. After that, if you want to stop the Spotlight placement, I can remove it before the next billing cycle. [CASEY: confirm actual cancellation timing/process before sending.]

**Q: What happens after the first 30 days?**

**Q: Are there multiple businesses in the same category or just one?**

## Section 5 — Send protocol

- Use the YES handler immediately after any clear affirmative reply, even short replies like "sounds good" or "send details."
- Use the NO handler once only. Do not continue follow-ups after a direct decline.
- Use the Q&A responses selectively — paste individual answers into personal replies instead of sending the whole section at once.
- Track outreach status in a simple pipeline: `SENT → FOLLOW-UP 1 → FOLLOW-UP 2 → YES / NO / QUESTIONS / CLOSED`.
- Escalate any YES reply that includes unusual requests, exclusivity demands, or custom placement questions before confirming anything.
- After sending the YES handler, move the business into a pending-launch list until all business details arrive.
