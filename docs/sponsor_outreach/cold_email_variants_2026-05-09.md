## Send protocol

1. Pick a business from the enrichment-sprint priority list.
2. Pick the matching email variant.
3. Replace `[Business Name]` (3–4 occurrences per email).
4. Replace the bracketed personalization placeholder (1 per email).
5. Pick a subject line (use the suggested one or write your own — the urgent-care subject is the only one that came pre-written).
6. Send from a personal email address.
7. Track in a spreadsheet: business name, category, sent date, response (none / yes / decline / asks-questions), follow-up date.

If response rate to a variant is < 5% after 20 sends in that category, rewrite it. If response rate is > 15%, scale it.
