Hava is different because the user is already asking a direct intent-based question like "best casual dinner near the London Bridge" or "kid-friendly lunch spot after boating." Sponsors appear naturally inside those answers with local context. It feels more like a trusted local recommendation than a directory listing, especially for visitors who don't know the area yet.

### Tier recommendation
