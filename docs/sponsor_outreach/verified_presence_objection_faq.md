# Verified Presence — Extended Objection-Handling FAQ

> **Companion to:** `docs/sponsor_outreach/verified_presence_pitch.md` §6.4. The pitch doc has a core 8-objection list embedded in the 3-minute sit-down script; this file is the **extended FAQ** Casey uses for prep, written replies, and post-pitch reference. Drafted via ChatGPT 2026-05-13, voice-anchored against the pitch doc; Cowork-primary polished and structured. `[CASEY: ...]` markers are unfilled — replace before each pitch run where applicable.

## 1. "We already get plenty of business from word of mouth."

**Response:**
That's honestly the best position to be in. I'm not trying to replace that. What I'm building is another place locals and visitors check when they need something fast — especially newer residents, tourists, or people searching after hours. If somebody asks Hava "who's a reliable plumber in Havasu," I'd rather local businesses show up than random national directories.

**If they still push back:**
Totally fair. If you're already slammed and don't need visibility right now, I'd rather you say no than pay for something you won't use.

---

## 2. "Send me an email and I'll think about it."

**Response:**
Absolutely. I'll send a short overview — nothing long or corporate. Mainly just what the listing includes, the pricing, and a couple screenshots so you can see what I'm talking about without trying to remember the whole conversation.

**If they still push back:**
No problem. If I don't hear back, I'll probably follow up once and then leave you alone.

---

## 3. "I'm not the decision maker."

**Response:**
Got it. Who usually handles this stuff — owner, spouse, office manager? I can either send you something simple to forward over, or I can stop by when they're around so I'm not putting you in the middle of it.

**If they still push back:**
No worries. Even just telling me who I should talk to saves me from bothering the wrong person.

---

## 4. "We're already on Google, Yelp, and Facebook."

**Response:**
You should be. This isn't meant to replace any of those. The difference is this is local-only and category-focused for Lake Havasu. Instead of competing against nationwide search clutter, you're showing up inside a product specifically built around people asking local questions like "who actually answers the phone after hours" or "who do locals trust for pool repair."

**If they still push back:**
Honestly, if Google and referrals already cover everything you need, that may be enough. I'm mainly talking to businesses that want another local visibility layer without spending thousands a month.

---

## 5. "What's the catch? Why is it only $79?"

**Response:**
There really isn't one. I'm early. I'd rather keep pricing low and get real businesses into the system than charge a bunch upfront before the product earns it. I'm founder-led right now, so there's no sales staff or agency overhead baked into the price.

**If they still push back:**
If this turns into a giant corporate thing someday, pricing probably changes. But right now I'm focused on getting useful local businesses onboard and learning what actually works.

---

## 6. "Can you guarantee leads?"

**Response:**
No. I won't say that because I can't control who searches, what people need, or who they call. What I can promise is you'll have a verified presence inside the platform, your information will be accurate, and you'll appear when people ask relevant local questions.

**If they still push back:**
Anybody guaranteeing leads at this stage is probably overselling you.

---

## 7. "How many people actually use this thing?"

**Response:**
Still early. I'm not going to pretend this has massive numbers yet. What I can tell you is the goal is building something genuinely useful for Havasu locals first instead of inflating metrics. I'd rather grow slower with real engagement than throw fake traffic numbers around.

**If they still push back:**
If you'd rather wait until the user base is bigger, I completely understand.

---

## 8. "We tried something like this before and it didn't work."

**Response:**
I hear that a lot. Most local advertising products overpromise, lock people into contracts, or disappear after six months. I'm trying to build something simpler: accurate local listings, searchable categories, and a chat tool people actually use when they need something nearby.

**If they still push back:**
Honestly, skepticism is reasonable. I'd probably feel the same way if I owned the business.

---

## 9. "I don't trust AI. Those chatbots make stuff up."

**Response:**
That's a valid concern. That's actually why the verified listing matters. The goal is to ground answers in real business information that you control — hours, services, contact info, photos, descriptions. I'd rather the system say "I don't know" than invent things.

**If they still push back:**
That's also why I'm doing local-first instead of trying to answer the entire internet.

---

## 10. "Is this a contract?"

**Response:**
No contracts. Month-to-month. Cancel anytime. If you try it for a while and decide it's not worth it, you stop.

**If they still push back:**
I'm not interested in trapping businesses into subscriptions they resent paying for.

---

## 11. "What happens if I cancel?"

**Response:**
Your verified placement and sponsor features come off, but your business may still exist in the directory as a standard listing if there's public information available. You just wouldn't have the verified badge or owner-controlled features anymore.

**If they still push back:**
I try to keep it straightforward — no weird cancellation process.

---

## 12. "Do I get to see what's on the listing before it goes live?"

**Response:**
Yes. The whole point is that you control the information. I'm not trying to build a review ambush site where random stuff goes live without context. I want businesses to be able to keep their info accurate and current.

**If they still push back:**
If something's wrong, we fix it. Pretty simple.

---

## 13. "What if my competitor signs up too?"

**Response:**
That'll probably happen in some categories. I'm not selling exclusivity right now. The goal is helping locals find good businesses, not pretending there's only one plumber or HVAC company in town.

**If they still push back:**
I'd rather be honest about that upfront than sell fake scarcity.

---

## 14. "I don't have time to manage another account."

**Response:**
Totally understandable. Most businesses barely have time to answer phones some days. That's why I'm keeping this lightweight — basic business info, photos, services, hours. Not daily posting or social-media-style upkeep.

**If they still push back:**
Honestly, if it starts becoming another full-time marketing chore, then I failed at designing it correctly.

---

## 15. "How is this different from Angi or HomeAdvisor?"

**Response:**
Different model. I'm not selling pay-per-lead forms or dumping your phone number into a bidding war. This is more like a local discovery layer for Havasu — directory plus conversational search — where businesses are surfaced because they're relevant locally, not because they bought the most aggressive lead package.

**If they still push back:**
I'm also not trying to become a national lead-generation machine. The local focus matters.

---

## 16. "What if nobody uses the chat feature?"

**Response:**
Then the directory still needs to stand on its own. I'm building both intentionally because some people want to browse categories manually and some people just want to ask a question. I don't think local search stays keyword-only forever, though.

**If they still push back:**
That's part of why I'm keeping the pricing reasonable while the platform matures.

---

## 17. "I already pay too much for advertising."

**Response:**
That's fair. A lot of local businesses are getting hammered with recurring marketing bills. I'm not asking you to replace your existing stuff overnight — this is more of a low-risk local presence play than a giant ad spend.

**If they still push back:**
If the extra $79/month creates stress instead of opportunity, I'd rather you pass for now.

---

## 18. "Can customers leave reviews on this thing?"

**Response:**
Eventually there may be feedback systems, but I'm being careful with that because local businesses already deal with enough garbage review dynamics online. Right now the focus is accurate information, discoverability, and verified presence.

**If they still push back:**
I'm more interested in usefulness than turning this into another review-war platform.

---

## 19. "How long is this going to be around?"

**Response:**
Fair question. I'm local, I'm building it myself, and I'm treating it like a real business — not a quick flip project. But I'm also not going to pretend I can predict the future. I'm building it because Havasu genuinely needs a better local discovery tool.

**If they still push back:**
Honestly, I think local businesses deserve products built by people who actually live here.

---

## 20. "I need to talk to my spouse/business partner first."

**Response:**
Completely reasonable. I can send a simple breakdown so you don't have to relay everything from memory. Usually once people see the actual listing format and pricing, they know pretty quickly whether it makes sense for them.

**If they still push back:**
No pressure from me. I'd rather everybody be comfortable with it before signing up.
